The app

ThumbDeck is a control that sits over supported video apps and lets you rewind, skip and change volume with one thumb. It depends on how those apps are built, and an update to another app can change what ThumbDeck is able to do there. We work to keep ThumbDeck compatible but cannot guarantee it will work in every app, video or phone.

Free trial and plans

ThumbDeck includes a free 7-day trial of all features, with no payment details needed and nothing charged when it ends. After the trial, volume control stays free, and rewind and skip need a paid plan:
