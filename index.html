Questions

Good to know.

Does it work on iPhone?

Sorry, Apple users. ThumbDeck only works on Android.

Why does it need Accessibility?

Android's Accessibility service is what lets ThumbDeck find a video's progress bar and move it for you. ThumbDeck uses it only in the video apps it supports, and never records or sends your screen.

What happens after the free trial?

Volume keeps working for free. Rewind and skip need a plan. Nothing is charged automatically when the trial ends.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchase back?

Install ThumbDeck with the same Google account, open the plans screen and tap Restore purchase.
