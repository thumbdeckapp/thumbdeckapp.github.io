Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

ThumbDeck is an Android app that lets you rewind, skip and change volume in video apps with one thumb. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: ThumbDeck does not collect, store or share your personal data.

What ThumbDeck collects

Nothing. ThumbDeck has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. It does not send any information about you or your phone anywhere.

How ThumbDeck uses Accessibility

ThumbDeck uses Android's Accessibility service so it can work inside the video apps it supports (Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube). With it, ThumbDeck:

  • checks which app is open, so the control only shows in supported video apps;
  • finds the video's progress bar on screen and performs the swipe that moves it when you use the control;
  • may briefly look at the screen to find where the video's playhead is. This happens on your phone only. Nothing is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere.

ThumbDeck does not read your messages, record your screen, collect what you type or see, or use Accessibility for anything else.

Purchases

Plans are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and ThumbDeck only receives confirmation of whether you own a plan. Google's privacy policy applies to that payment.

Settings

Your ThumbDeck settings and trial start date are stored on your phone only and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Problem reports

The app can copy a problem report to your clipboard. It contains app settings and recent technical events, no personal information, and it only leaves your phone if you choose to paste and send it to us.

Children

ThumbDeck is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
